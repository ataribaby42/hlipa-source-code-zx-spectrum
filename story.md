# The Story of HLÍPA

HLÍPA is a member of the vast HLÍPA nation, which lives on the planet Danepa.
This planet orbits the star Tsivoke, hidden from us observers on Earth
somewhere behind opaque clouds of cosmic dust at the centre of our galaxy.

The HLÍPA nation came into being many millions of years ago. Its members
are a kind of “amoeba”: shapeless, intelligent creatures that move by flowing
from one place to another.

But the members of the HLÍPA nation are not the only inhabitants of Danepa.
Another nation lives there too: the Sivrams. Unlike the HLÍPA nation,
Sivram society is made up of more than one species. It is divided into
Falmons, mindless, chimerical creatures with limited intellect, bred for work,
and Ploxons, which cannot move but possess highly developed intelligence.
These two species live in symbiosis and wage war together against the HLÍPA nation.

The Falmons are particularly dangerous: even a fleeting touch dries out
the bodies of the HLÍPA people, draining their lives away. Ploxons, on the
other hand, have no defence against them, and their bodies disintegrate
at a touch from HLÍPA. The HLÍPA people cannot destroy Falmons, so the only
way to defeat the Sivrams is to rid their nation of its Ploxons. The Falmons
will then be unable to sustain their nation on their own and will eventually
scatter to remote parts of the planet, where they will die of sorrow and old age.

The Sivrams’ capital “city” is a single vast network of corridors and rooms
carved into the rock deep beneath the planet’s surface. It is home to the six
ruling Ploxons. Despite all the dangers, the HLÍPA people have managed to send
one of their number inside and have tasked her with destroying these six
Ploxons. Your task is to help her, for without help she will surely perish
in the underground palace.

Above all, beware of the Falmons patrolling the palace’s many rooms.
Ploxons are easy to recognise: their bodies resemble small royal crowns
floating just above the ground. HLÍPA can crawl along the palace floors
or use the intricate network of channels the Falmons have built there.
